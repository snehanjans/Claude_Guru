# Program share / meta images (og:image)

Drop each program's banner here with these exact filenames. They render as the
link-preview image in the Social Media Kit (LinkedIn & WhatsApp previews). A
missing file falls back to the "GREAT LEARNING" brand gradient.

- `ai-native-professional.png` — flagship ("Build agents in 6 weeks without coding")
- `ai-native-professional-for-hr.png` — "Automate your daily HR work with AI tools"
- `ai-native-professional-for-marketing.png` — "Automate your daily marketing work with AI tools"
- `ai-native-professional-for-finance.png` — "Automate your daily finance work with AI tools"

Recommended size: 1200 × 627 (the LinkedIn/OG standard). PNG or re-save as PNG
to keep the filename. Files are served by Vite from `/og/<name>.png`.
